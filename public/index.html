Run the show

  1. Spin the wheel. It picks a random song, and everyone sees the same spin.
  2. Listen together on Discord while people vote. Hosts get a player under the song title on the stage.
  3. Close voting to lock the score, then spin again.

The wheel stopped but voting did not open?

This only happens if every host page was closed during a spin. Pick the song the wheel landed on and confirm it.
